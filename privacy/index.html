Privacy & cookies

Privacy policy.

What this site collects, what Google’s advertising does, and how to opt out. Last updated 11 October 2026.

Who we are

Eye To Ad Media (Search Converts LLC) runs localfoodnearme.com from 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, CO 80204. Send any question about this page through the contact page or by calling 1-800-481-8638.

What we collect

Forms. If you send the contact or listing form, we receive what you type: your name, restaurant, email, phone, city and message. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our inbox. It is used to answer you and to deal with your listing, nothing else: no selling, no mailing list.

Directory searches. The search runs in your browser. It downloads our public data files and filters them on your device; we do not store what you search for.

Server logs. Like any website, our host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Google ads and cookies

Pages on this site carry Google AdSense advertising. To choose which ads to show, Google and other third-party vendors place and read cookies in your browser, and those cookies can reflect pages you looked at here earlier as well as on other websites. Through its advertising cookies, Google and the partners it works with can show you ads informed by your visits to localfoodnearme.com and to other sites across the web.

Personalised ads can be switched off in Google’s ad settings. Many other ad vendors honour the opt-out tool run by the Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info/choices. Google explains its own practices on its page about data from partner websites and apps.

In places whose privacy laws call for it, such as the EU, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google may appear first, and ad cookies are only set if you agree. You can change your browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time; the site still works without them.

Other third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to restaurant websites, award bodies and news sources take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Children

The site is written for adults. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not set out to gather any information about them.

Your choices

Ask us, and we will tell you what form details of yours are on file or erase them, through the contact page. California residents can make the same requests under the CCPA; we do not sell personal information.

Changes

Whenever this policy is revised, the “last updated” line near the top is revised too.
